## Item 24: evidence that ThomasAM_2019_c comes from the Yachida 2019 cohort

I found no document that says in words "ThomasAM_2019_c = Yachida 2019 samples". The evidence below is a chain of records:

1. **cMD curation table.** In `waldronlab/curatedMetagenomicDataCuration`, `inst/curated/ThomasAM_2019_c/ThomasAM_2019_c_sample.tsv` lists 80 samples: 40 "Case" (colorectal carcinoma) and 40 "Study Control". The fields are `country = Japan`, `pmid = 30936548` (Thomas 2019) and `sequencing_platform = IlluminaHiSeq`. Sample IDs are flow-cell or barcode strings such as `AHCNC2ADXX.N708_CAGAGAGG`. `inst/extdata/studies_status.csv` lists ThomasAM_2019_c (80 samples, Japan, no run accessions) and YachidaS_2019 (616 samples, PMID 31171880) as **separate** studies.
2. **Thomas 2019 [3], Methods (PMC9533319).** "Validation Cohort2 consists of 40 CRC samples and 40 controls from a Japanese cohort from Tokyo." Funding comes from National Cancer Center grants. Yachida, Shibata, Mizutani, Shiroma and Yamada are co-authors. The paper does not give an accession or a citation to Yachida 2019 for this cohort.
3. **Wirbel 2019 [2] (PMC7984229).** It uses the same type of Japanese validation cohort ("JP, N = 40 CRC, N = 40 CTR") with the same NCC funding. Its Data Availability section says: "The independent validation cohorts can be found ... in the DDBJ database under the ID DRA006684."
4. **Yachida 2019 [20], Nature article page.** "The raw sequencing data reported in this paper have been deposited in DDBJ Sequence Read Archive (DRA) as DRA006684 and DRA008156."
5. **ENA.** BioProject PRJDB4176 (Tokyo Institute of Technology) holds 80 runs under DRA006684 and 565 runs under DRA008156. Its description reads "fecal samples of Japanese patients who underwent colonoscopy at National Cancer Research Center".

**Conclusion.** The 80-sample Japanese validation cohort used in Thomas 2019 and Wirbel 2019 was deposited as DRA006684. DRA006684 is one of the two DRA submissions that Yachida 2019 names as its own data. That makes ThomasAM_2019_c very likely a subset of the Yachida 2019 (PRJDB4176) cohort.

**Caveats:**
- I could not match the cMD sample IDs to DRR run IDs. None of the 80 cMD IDs appear in the ENA run or sample aliases for DRA006684, whose sample titles look like "human feces_10322".
- Sample-level identity is therefore not proven.
- GitHub issues on duplicate samples could not be opened. Search showed curatedMetagenomicData #120 and curatedMetagenomicDataCuration #14 and #92 ("Duplicated records present across studies"), but issue access was blocked in this session.
- If both ThomasAM_2019_c and YachidaS_2019 are used, check them for possible double counting.
