# 2026-11-24 — HYPOTHESIS / FAILURE (cycle 5, DEV only)
HYPOTHESIS (before running): if over-flagging comes from taxa that differ between healthy populations, then a
classifier that down-weights such taxa (penalty by their between-population ICC among unlabelled healthy DEV cohorts,
cross-fitted) will show less FPR spread across held-out DEV populations with AUROC within 0.02.
RESULT (v5/results/dev_gsp.csv): no consistent gain. Mean |FPR-0.10| CRC LASSO 0.066 (none) vs 0.072-0.080 (penalised);
IBD LASSO 0.112 vs 0.108-0.118 (penalty) but 0.077 when half the taxa are dropped (AUROC 0.769 -> 0.736); T2D LASSO
0.103 vs 0.076 (drop half) ; RF mixed. FPR SD across populations barely moves.
DIAGNOSIS: ICC estimated on one half of the DEV populations does not identify the taxa that shift the OTHER half; the
shift is population-specific (which taxa matter depends on which population), so a global penalty cannot target it.
DEAD END for a "geography-robust classifier" via feature penalties. TURN: the useful product is the atlas itself,
broadened to many disease tests, as a resource; plus a per-site check.
